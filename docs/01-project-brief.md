# Project Brief

## Objective

Create NUVAIX GROUP's premium digital headquarters: a trustworthy, sophisticated, scalable corporate website that presents the group as a diversified, technology-driven enterprise.

## Primary audiences

- Customers
- Contractors
- Property developers
- Businesses
- Technology clients
- Training participants
- Suppliers
- Strategic partners
- Investors / prospective investors
- Job applicants
- Public institutions

## Primary conversions

1. Business enquiry
2. Partnership enquiry
3. Technology-services enquiry
4. Paint/product enquiry
5. Training enquiry
6. Career application
7. General contact

## Experience goal

The first impression should feel:

- premium
- credible
- modern
- enterprise-grade
- technologically capable
- distinctly NUVAIX

## Core storytelling

Hero -> Who We Are -> Business Divisions -> Technology -> NUVAIX Paint -> Projects -> Leadership -> Partnerships -> Contact.

Do not overwhelm the homepage with every corporate detail. Use strong summaries and deep internal pages.
